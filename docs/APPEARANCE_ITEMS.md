# APPEARANCE ITEMS: cosmetics and body modifications

Status: first pass (procedural rendering). Source of truth for the data is `src/content/appearance.ts`; the tables in section 6 are generated by
`node tools/gen_appearance_doc.mjs` (do not hand-edit them). Lore follows docs/UNIVERSE.md: the three manufacturers (Harrow-Brandt Heavy Works,
Aldane Surgical, Kestrel Value Systems), the bleak industrial Verge Basin, restrained accents, no corporate villains.

## 1. What this is

Appearance items change how the character LOOKS and nothing else. They have no stats (the type has no stats field; a test asserts that equipping a full look
leaves `computeBuild` stats identical). They live in a separate **Wardrobe** (`save.cosmetics`: owned + one equipped item per slot), not in the hardware
locker, so they never compete for locker capacity and cannot interfere with the recommender, sell/dispose flows, bank or hardware tests.

* **104 items** across **12 slots**: Hair, Headgear, Eyes, Torso, Arms, Hands, Legs, Back, Skin & body mods, Tattoos & marks, Aura, Trail.
* Counts per rarity: grey 20, green 27, blue 28, purple 22, orange 7.
* 11 items (the Harrow / Aldane / Kestrel "pattern" torso, arms, hands, legs) reuse the **existing body-part variant art** (`src/bodyvariants.ts`): equipping one swaps that body part's prebaked layer set without installing hardware. The other 93 are drawn procedurally (`src/cosmetics.ts`) around the animated sprite until bespoke art exists.

## 2. Rarity mapping (reconciled with the existing scheme)

The game already has five rarities in `src/config.ts`: `grey, green, blue, purple, orange` (colours #b4b6b2, #93b676, #82aed2, #b79ccb, #dd8d42) with orange as the top
rank, used for the signature boss items. Sean's ordering for appearance is: grey/white common, green and blue aspirational, **purple top tier**, **orange extremely rare**.
These are compatible, so **no rename and no new tier**: the existing colours and ranks are reused unchanged.

| Colour | Meaning for cosmetics | Where it comes from | Share of a boss cosmetic roll |
|---|---|---|---|
| grey | Common, everyday, cheap | Vendor (50-120c), any mob | ~8% |
| green | Aspirational, a visible step up | Vendor (420-520c), elites, drops | ~38% |
| blue | Aspirational, distinctive | Vendor (1.5-1.75kc), elites, drops | ~38% |
| purple | **Top practical tier**: the build-defining look | Late and specific **boss** drops, 12-clear achievement | ~15.5% weight, only from boss kills |
| orange | **Extremely rare**: legends | Event roll (about 0.2% on any boss kill, +25% per dungeon tier) or marathon achievement. Never sold. Never from mobs or elites | 0.12 weight, orange is never part of the normal table |

Justification: orange already denotes the single rarest hardware (weight .05 in the normal loot pools, signature-only for bosses), so it stays the rarest here, and
purple is the rarest tier a player can reasonably farm. Orange is also excluded from the generic weighted table entirely: it can only come from an `event` or `ach` source, so it can never
be diluted by pity or farming. Purple is boss-gated (any boss for a handful, a specific boss for most), which gives bosses a cosmetic identity worth repeating.

### 2.1 Drop mechanics (src/content/appearance.ts, wired in sim.rollLoot)

* Cosmetics go **straight into the wardrobe** with a toast; there is no ground pile. So they add no loot-pile volume and **DROP_RATE_SCALE and the existing drop tests are unaffected**. They are an extra, separate roll after the gear rolls.
* Chance a kill rolls a cosmetic at all: ordinary mob 0.4%, elite 5%, boss 35%. Boss-specific purple/blue item: +12% per kill (+10% per dungeon tier).
* Rarity weights (grey/green/blue/purple): ordinary 70/24/5.5/0.4, elite 38/38/18/5.5, boss 8/38/38/15.5. Purple weight rises 15% per dungeon tier.
* Duplicates never roll when an unowned item of that rarity exists; if everything of the rolled rarity is owned, the roll pays credits instead (grey 10c, green 40c, blue 150c, purple 600c, orange 4000c).
* Achievements (guaranteed, idempotent): clear 12 different dungeons (purple tattoo), clear all 24 (orange cowl).
* Vendor: the Wardrobe lists grey/green/blue items from the counter (priced in credits). Purple and orange are never sold.

## 4. Slots

Hair, Headgear, Eyes, Torso, Arms, Hands, Legs, Back, Skin & body mods (chrome, lit veins, ceramic glaze, rust, frost, hologram), Tattoos & marks, Aura, Trail. One item per slot.
Slots map to the sprite rig: torso/arms/hands/legs may use `variant` (real art); everything else is an overlay anchored to the rig (feet at the origin, ~48 units tall).

## 5. Systems

* **Data:** `src/content/appearance.ts` (typed `CosDef`, `COSMETICS`, `rollCosmetic`, `buyCos`, `checkAchievements`, `CosState` in the save).
* **Body variants:** `src/bodyvariants.ts` `applyCosmeticVariants` layers equipped cosmetic `variant`s on top of the hardware-derived body (hardware look is unchanged when nothing is equipped).
* **Rendering:** `src/cosmetics.ts` draws procedural looks in two phases around the player sprite (back: aura, back gear, trail; front: skin, clothing, marks, hair, headgear, eyes). The renderer calls it from `drawPlayer`.
* **UI:** Locker header **Wardrobe** button -> `src/wardrobeui.ts`. Progressive disclosure: owned and vendor items only by default, a "Show all" toggle for the rest, undiscovered items show as "?" tiles with slot, rarity and source hint only (name, look and lore are hidden). Icon grid with slot tabs, hover tooltips (name, rarity, theme, look, lore, source), a live animated preview using the real player sprite with the selected item applied before you commit, a worn-slots strip (click to unequip), collected counts per rarity, Equip/Unequip/Buy actions.
* **Tests:** `tests/appearance.mjs` (data, rarity policy, stat neutrality, body-variant art, UI, preview, persistence, every style paints).
